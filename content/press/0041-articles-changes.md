As technology moves forward, we are eager to explore every aspect of it, including the aspects that were recently introduced. We are progressing further in technology, and we have to move as quickly as the evolution of tech. One of the progresses is publishing technology news to our Aptivi Blog alongside our projects to make sure that you're up to date.

However, we'd like to improve on this area, because some of our articles weren't meeting our high bar for quality content, and we apologize if that caused some confusion. As a resukt, we are planning to introduce the following changes:

  * **Software update articles**: When we spot an evidence of new software updates, such as major or minor updates, being rolled out to devices and/or software that we currently track, we often make a single article for a single variant of a device. Since this practice was considered to be a "quantity is superior than quality", which clearly isn't what we're aiming for, we've decided to consolidate such news to a daily roundup, should updates exist.
  * **Product updates and releases**: For more consistency in our writing style and to introduce various ways to introduce users to product updates and releases, we've decided to improve the phrasing for each article so that our articles become easier to grasp and understand for both tech-savvy people and for the non-tech-savvy ones for low-tech articles.
  * **Other articles**: For other tech articles that we plan to make, we'll improve the way we deliver our articles, such as tech updates and other updates, to ensure our authenticity is visible, not repetition.

The appropriate changes will be made starting September 13th as part of our effort to ensure that our articles meet the high quality bar. This way, we'll have improved our blog's content, and hopefully increase our visibility.
