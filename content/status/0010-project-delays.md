We have earlier announced the incident report concerning our network connection that can be found [here](https://officialaptivi.wordpress.com/2026/09/03/aptivi-project-development-incident-announcement-as-of-9-2-2026/). It negatively impacted our ability to continue development of oir projects due to no access to interner connection.

As we have mentioned earlier, our projects have been assigned new release dates to align with the lost development time resulting from lack of proper internet access, which was a whole week. This means that some of the releases that we have planned won't align with our older deadlines that we mentioned in some documents, such as public articles that were released to both Aptivi Blog and Aptivi Newsroom, which explicitly mentioned the exact release date that got invalidated due to network incidents.

We have coordinated with our internet service provider to examine the situation, and appropriate actions will be taken to solve the network connectivity issues. Due to the volatility of the situation, we are unable to provide exact information, but we'll keep you updated in case we hear updates from the service provider.

We will release Terminaux 8.8 on October 1st, Nitrocid 0.2.1 Release Candidate on October 8th, and Nitrocid 0.2.1 Final on November 5th. Meanwhile, we will continue to evaluate the situation to ensure that something worse won't happen.

We will continue development of our projects, but slightly slower than before.
