+++

Since the last support status change, we have obtained One UI 9.0 on one of our devices. This resulted in us finally providing official support for those who are running our projects on Samsung phones and tablets running One UI 9.0, which is based on Android 17.

The support policy has been updated so that One UI 9.0 is officially supported, with the support timeline being shared with One UI 8.x and 7.x, which is selected as December 2nd, 2032.

Additionally, we have bumped the Android version requirement to Android 14, which means that support for Android 13, 12, and 11 have officially ended for our projects. Our official support stays intact in most of the conditions, though.

After revising the support policy, the following conditions must be met:

  * The manufacturer must be able to provide security updates to their Android devices for the minimum of 3 years.
  * The manufacturer must be able to provide at least 2 Android version upgrades.
  * Your device must be manufactured after 2023 with an ARM64 processor.
  * We don't officially offer support for Android 13 or lower
  * We don't officially offer support for non-Samsung devices (Google Pixel, Huawei, Xiaomi, etc.)
  * We don't officially offer support for Android versions that we don't have access to yet

The documentation will be updated in the next docs update cycle for MediaBoom 1.0, Nitrocid 0.2.1, and Terminaux 8.9.
